# A Robot Sandwich
Given precise instructions the robot will make a peanut butter and jelly sandwich 
### Assumptions Section
The robot is considered a humanoid due to it's anatomic nature being cloned from human anatomy. When referring to body parts that can be found on the majority of the human species it is assumed that the robot also has those parts and the robot is aware of how to use those parts if given clear instruction on what to do with them. The ingredients and utensil (butter knife) necessary to make a peanut butter and jelly sandwich are present in the kitchen and are already laid out on the countertop. The specific ingredients on the countertop are: a jar of grape jelly and a jar of peanut butter both have already had the freshness seal and the lids removed and 2 sandwich size slices of bread roughly 4.5 inches wide by 4.5 inches tall and about .5 inches thick. Both slices of bread are laying flat on the countertop. To eliminate confusion, laying flat means the broad side is down and the crust creates the entire perimeter of the slice so you can only see one side of each slice. The side of each slice that is facing up and is visible can be noted as side 1. The side that is not visible at the moment that is facing down against the countertop surface is noted as side 2. The 2 slices of bread will be noted as Slice A and Slice B. Slice A is to the left of Slice B, both slices are separated by 2 inches, meaning between slice A there is 2 inches of visible countertop in between. Slice A and Slice B have all their corresponding edges aligned parellel to each other. Neither slice is overlapping, touching, or standing on it's crust edges. As of this time the robots hands are empty. Although the robot lacks common sense, it has an abundance of vocabular knowledge and can identify all items (ingredients and utensil) correctly that are on the countertop as well as identify all of its body parts correctly. The robot is standing at the counter where the ingredients and the butter knife are located and can reach all of the items if told to do something with them. The direction the robot is currently facing will be called the "front", the counter to that would be 180 degrees from "front" is called "back". The left will be noted as the robot's anatomical left or 90 degrees counterclockwise from front. Right is noted as the robot's anatomical right or 90 degrees clockwise from front. The directions up and down are known by differentiating which way gravity is pulling. "Up" is noted as the opposite direction of gravity, "down" is noted as the direction gravity is pulling towards. The robot is familiar with all units of measurement both imperial and metric systems. 
### Step by Step List
1. Visualize the butter knife on the countertop
2. Grasp the butter knife with your right hand by placing the handle, which is the end opposite of the bladed portion, on top of your 4 fingers and then wrapping your thumb over the top securing it in your grip. 
3. Move your right hand with the knife still secured direclty above the peanut butter jar. 
4. With the bladed end of the knife, submerge it approximately 1 inch into the peanut butter substance by entering from the top of the jar. 
5. With the bladed end still submerged, move your right hand to the right until there is roughly 1 tablespoon of peanut butter adhering to the knife.
6. Remove the knife from the peanut butter by lifting your right hand directly up, ensuring that the 1 tablespoon is still adhering to the butter knife. keeping the knife horizontal to the surface and the peanut butter facing up towards the ceiling. If it falls off, repeat steps 3-5.
7. Kepping hold of the knife and ensuring the peanut butter is still on the knife, move your right hand directly above Slice A
8. Lower your right hand till the bladed end of the knife with the peanut butter is approximately .5 inches above Slice A
9. Rotate the knife so the peanut butter is now facing down towards Slice A, if the peanut butter falls off it should fall onto Slice A given the approximate distance to the bread. 
10. Lower your right hand with the knife until it makes contact with the breaded portion of Slice A. 
11. Using your right hand spread the peanut butter around slice A, side 1, only on the breaded portion which is the inner portion that is surrounded by crust on all edges. 
12. Using your right hand with the knife still in your hand, move the bladed end of the knife approximately 1 inch above the opened jelly container
13. Using the bladed end of the knife submerge it approximately 1 inch into the jelly by entering from the top opening of the jelly container.
14. With the bladed end submerged, move your right hand approximately 1 inch to the right gathering 1 tablespoon of jelly on the broad side of the bladed end of the knife.
15. Rotate the knife so that the broad side with the jelly adhered to it is facing up towards the ceiling.
16. Keeping the knife horizontal with the broad side of the bladed end that has the jelly on it facing up, move your hand approximately .5 inches above Slice B side 1
17. Rotate the knife so that the broad side with the jelly is now facing down
18. Lower your knife with the broad side still facing down until you come into contact with side 1 of Slice B
19. Using your right hand spread the jelly around Slice B side 2, only on the breaded portion which is the inner portion that is surrounded by crust on all edges. 
20. Using your right hand lift it approximately 2 feet upwards
21. Using your right hand move it approximately 2 feet to the right ensuring that it is still above the countertop
22. Lower your right hand with the knife still in your hand until it comes into contact with the counter top. 
23. Rotate your hand so that your palm is facing down towards the countertop.
24. Open your right hand so that the knife falls onto the countertop.
25. With your left hand open move it to approximately 1 inch away from the bottom left corner of Slice A keeping your hand in contact with the countertop.
26. Using minimal pressure using the ring finger and middle finger of your left hand to make contact with the bottom left corner of side 2 on Slice A and use your thumb to make contact with the bottom left corner of side 1 of Slice A.
27. Raise Slice A up and rotate it on it's horizontal axis so the bottom left corner of Slice A should now look like it is the bottom right corner.
28. Lower Slice A down onto Side 1 of Slice B so that all corners of the slices align together. Slice A side 2 should now be facing up and you can no longer see side 1 or 2 of Slice B.
### Done When
- The peanut butter and jelly are in contact with each other
- Corresponding corners of Slice A and Slice B are all touching each other with no overlap
